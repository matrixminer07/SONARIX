Built for the full survey pipeline

Automated debris detection

Classifies debris and marine objects directly from side-scan sonar imagery, no manual frame-by-frame review.

Unknown anomaly flagging

Surfaces patterns that don't fit known classes so a human can make the final call, instead of silently discarding them.

Geotagged reporting

Confidence scoring

About Sonarix

Mission control for marine debris survey.

SONARIX is an AI-powered platform for automated detection of marine debris and unknown anomalies in side-scan sonar imagery. It helps survey operators, environmental teams, and AUV mission planners reduce manual review time and prioritize cleanup operations.

SONAR·NAVIGATION·AND·RECOVERY·INTELLIGENCE·EXPLORER
  • Automated debris detection and classification
  • Unknown anomaly flagging for human review
  • Geotagged report generation
  • Confidence scoring and uncertainty visualization
  • Responsive dashboard for desktop and mobile
